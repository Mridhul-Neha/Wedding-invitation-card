Our Journey

A Love Story We Never Planned

The Classmates

Our story didn't start with love at first sight. We were just two students doing our MBA at Chinmaya Institute of Technology. We didn't know it then, but those normal college days were the beautiful start of our forever.

🎓
🃏

A Game of UNO & Bangalore Nights

Friendship began in the simplest way: an invitation to play UNO. We laughed and teased each other. During our Bangalore internship, Hangouts, chats, conversations, and random plans with our friends cemented a bond that grew stronger friendship between us.

The Certificate Journey

After college, a meet to collect our certificates from University changed things. A bike ride, an incident at her place where I stood up for her, and spending the whole day together made us realize this connection was special.

🏍️
❤️

When Friendship Became Love

Her unexpected proposals changed my whole world. Through the doubts of whether it was just affection, we found our truth. Our connection was too strong for just friendship, and we realized that the best of friends make the most beautiful lovers.

Building Together in Career

I loved making her happy with surprise office visits with cakes. Eventually, seeing her under pressure, I wanted better for her. She resigned and joined my company, Orgvein, as HR. We were now building a career and a life alongside each other.

🏢
✨

Forever

The classmates became friends, the friends became lovers, the lovers became partners, and soon... husband and wife. Because the greatest love stories aren't always perfectly planned; sometimes they happen quietly, between friends.

"Our story is not ending here. This is only the beginning of the next chapter."
